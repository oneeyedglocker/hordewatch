**Ping tells you who to hit first.**

It scans for nearby enemy players like Spy does, then goes further: it finds the
healer, tracks the cooldowns your targets have burned, and sorts the list so the
player worth killing is at the top. Built for world PvP and battlegrounds on TBC
Classic.

## What it does that Spy doesn't

**Finds the healer.** Not by class — by watching a player actually heal someone
else. Only spells from an editable whitelist count, so bandages, potions,
healthstones and Life Tap never mark a warlock as a healer. You set how many
heals, and how large, before Ping is convinced.

**Tracks enemy cooldowns.** When a target blows Cloak of Shadows, bubbles, or
trinkets out, Ping shows a live countdown on their row — so you know how long
you have and when it comes back. The watch list is yours; nothing is tracked
that you didn't add.

**Sorts by class.** Float the classes you care about to the top of the list, or
show only those classes. Warlocks first, or rogues only — your call.

**Kill priority.** Replaces "most recently seen" with "most worth attacking":
Kill-on-Sight, then confirmed healers, then whoever is actually in the fight.

**Healer-only view.** Hide everyone who can't heal. Cuts a mass fight down to the
targets that matter.

**14 themes.** Seven recolour the window; seven replace the artwork, control
icons, and (for Villain HUD) the fonts.

**And more:** Kill-on-Sight with reasons and guild flagging · stealth alerts ·
level floors for unknown enemies · nameplate and view-distance controls · a
250-entry encounter log per character · a configurable minimap button · TomTom
waypoints to a last-known position.

## Coming from Spy?

Ping installs **alongside** Spy — it has its own folder, saved variables and
settings, so nothing collides. **Data → Spy Import** brings your history across
in one click: your Kill-on-Sight list with reasons, your ignore list, and every
player you've ever seen — win/loss records, levels, guilds and all. Spy's own
data is never touched, and importing twice is safe.

## Getting started

1. Install and log in.
2. Type `/ping` or click the minimap button to open the window.
3. Options are under `/ping` → the Ping panel, or Interface → AddOns → Ping.
